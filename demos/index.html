cesium-helpers

cesium-binoculars

A camera mode that allows the user to use binoculars with the mouse wheel.

cesium-compass

A compass widget.

cesium-compass-bar

A horizontal compass bar widget showing the camera heading.

cesium-first-person-mode

A first person navigation mode that uses the Pointer Lock API.

cesium-flyto

A camera mode that flies toward the double-clicked position, with motion blur and depth of field.

cesium-path-flyover

A camera animation that follows a GPX or GeoJSON track.

cesium-post-process

Screen-space effects: Technicolor and Super 8 film looks, infrared, color isolation, tilt-shift, motion blur, speed lines, a spotlight, valley fog, a snow line, and a drone camera: wide lens, jello, display, analog and digital video.

cesium-sphere-camera

A camera mode that allows the user to rotate the camera around a position.

cesium-view-cube

A view cube widget.
